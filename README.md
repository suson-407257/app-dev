## 🍿 Favorite Movies

1. **[Inception](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inception)** (2010)
   - A heist that happens inside dreams
   - *Why I love it:* the ending still makes me argue with friends
2. **[Interstellar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstellar_(film))** (2014)
   - A space trip that is really about family
   - *Why I love it:* the music alone gives me chills
3. **[The Dark Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight)** (2008)
   - Batman versus the Joker at his scariest
   - *Why I love it:* the villain steals every scene
4. **[Parasite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parasite_(2019_film))** (2019)
   - Two families from opposite sides of the city
   - *Why I love it:* it keeps surprising you right up to the end
5. **[Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Into_the_Spider-Verse)** (2018)
   - Miles Morales becomes his own Spider-Man
   - *Why I love it:* the art style looks like a comic book come alive

## 📺 Favorite Series

- **[Breaking Bad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking_Bad)**
  - A chemistry teacher turns into something much darker
  - *Why I love it:* every episode makes the tension climb
- **[Stranger Things](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stranger_Things)**
  - Kids, monsters, and a lot of 80s nostalgia
  - *Why I love it:* the friend group feels so real
- **[Money Heist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Money_Heist)**
  - A mastermind plans the biggest robbery in Spain
  - *Why I love it:* the plans always have a backup plan
- **[Squid Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squid_Game)**
  - Desperate people play childhood games for a huge prize
  - *Why I love it:* it hits hard and makes you think
- **[Arcane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcane_(TV_series))**
  - Two sisters caught between two cities
  - *Why I love it:* the animation is gorgeous and the story hurts in a good way
